Sacred Wounds

Language matters. Harm is real.

Sacred Wounds is an art project examining the language used in religious spaces to control, exclude, and harm. Each piece dissects a phrase commonly heard in churches—what it says, what it means, and what people actually needed to hear instead.

This project exists because too many people have been told their questions, their identities, or their pain are incompatible with faith. The goal is not to attack belief, but to name the harm done when language is wielded as a weapon.
